There are now 415 images and 118 reviewed sex comparisons: 112 photographic comparisons plus six supplementary illustrated pairs. Full source URLs, per-file licenses, hashes, transformations and original-legend links are in `dist/photos.json`. Source descriptions are retained in `data/commons-photo-sources.json`; assignment evidence and unresolved/rejected candidates are recorded in `data/photo-sex-review.json`. Modified/rasterized illustration assets can be restored exactly from Git, rather than downloading their original PNG/PDF as though it were the displayed JPEG.

No sex assignment was inferred just from colors. The Dresser snowfinch candidate was rejected: the source describes two males in different seasonal plumages and a nestling. Dunkelwasserläufer candidates lacked explicit male/female assignment or mixed different species.

Illustrations retain the original photos, are marked “Historische Illustration”, identify the depicted sexes/other ages where verified, and show individual source and license links. “Bildnachweis” links to the original legend and discloses cropping/conversion. On narrow cards, illustrations use the full card width; the enlargement dialog shows both the photo and illustration. Historical idealization, color and seasonal limitations are stated in the app.

Validation: species/answer/repetition checks, lesson recovery, cache reuse, image provenance/sex assignment/hash checks and JavaScript syntax checks passed. A preservation check confirmed every preexisting photo record is unchanged and taxonomy, answer logic, storage and cache implementations have no changes. Browser QA confirmed no images before flashcard grading, both photo and illustration loaded after grading, source/sex captions and enlargement behavior. Layout at 320, 390 and 768 pixels showed no horizontal overflow. No new physical-device test was performed.
